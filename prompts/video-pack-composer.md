# Video pack composer

Compose prompts for external video tools.

Each segment should include:

- referenced keyframe images;
- subject action;
- exact shot alignment: no skipped, merged, duplicated, or reordered shots;
- duration timeline whose sub-ranges cover the full segment without overlap;
- main motion and secondary animation for each shot;
- focus path between subject, prop, and story signal;
- camera movement;
- transition bridge;
- continuity anchors;
- duration target;
- platform-specific notes.

Never claim Codex generated the final video.
