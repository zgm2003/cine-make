# Storyboard composer

Create still-image prompts, not motion prompts.

A strong storyboard prompt includes:

- subject anchor;
- scene anchor;
- character, scene, and prop preset locks;
- visible action;
- micro-performance;
- expression detail built from brows, eyes, mouth, breath, and hand tension;
- body action and a frozen secondary-animation cue;
- shot size;
- camera/lens feel;
- composition;
- lighting;
- palette;
- continuity anchors;
- negative constraints such as no text overlay and no watermark.

Keep it as a still-image prompt. Do not ask the image model to animate, infer the whole story, add unlisted characters, or change identity/costume/props.
