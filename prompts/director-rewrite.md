# Director rewrite

Rewrite source text into filmable beats.

Require:

- visible action;
- micro-performance;
- blocking;
- emotional transition;
- character intent;
- continuity anchors.

Rules:

- Convert inner monologue into playable behavior: eye line, breath, hand tension, posture, pause, or a reaction to a prop.
- Keep source order intact; do not add story turns just to make a shot prettier.
- Give clues, scares, reversals, and threshold decisions their own beat instead of burying them in transition action.
- Preserve character and object presets across pronouns, aliases, and family titles.

Do not add lore that is not needed for the target duration.
