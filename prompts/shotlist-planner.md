# Shotlist planner

Each shot must include:

- shot_id;
- duration_seconds;
- scene;
- subject;
- visible action;
- performance detail;
- shot size;
- camera movement;
- composition;
- lighting;
- dialogue or voiceover;
- image prompt;
- continuity from previous shot;
- video prompt note.

Planning rules:

- One shot should advance one visible action, clue, emotional turn, or continuity handoff.
- Do not skip, merge, duplicate, or reorder source beats.
- Give a separate shot to important clues, sudden reveals, reversals, and threshold choices.
- Replace generic emotion labels with concrete facial and body behavior.
- `video_prompt_note` must describe main motion, secondary animation, focus path, and what must not drift.
